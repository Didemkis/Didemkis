## 👋 About Me

> 🎯 With the goal of specializing in **cybersecurity**, I am actively developing myself through training, internships, and volunteer projects — currently focused on **Red Team & Web Application Security**, practicing through the **PortSwigger Web Security Academy**.

- 🎓 &nbsp;I hold degrees in **Management Information Systems** and **Computer Programming**, combining a technical background with analytical thinking and a process-oriented approach to security.
- 🔍 &nbsp;Previously gained experience in **SOC analysis**, **log analysis**, **IDS/IPS**, and **SIEM / SOAR** solutions with tools such as **Splunk** and **Wireshark** — now paired with an **offensive security** perspective.
- 🛡️ &nbsp;Also interested in **Information Security (ISMS / ISO 27001)**: how technical findings should be addressed in the context of **risk, control, and compliance**.
- ✍️ &nbsp;I write technical and educational content on **Medium**, and share PoCs, lab work, and security projects here on **GitHub**.
- 💡 &nbsp;A team player, open to learning, and a responsible cybersecurity enthusiast building a career with solid, conscious steps.
